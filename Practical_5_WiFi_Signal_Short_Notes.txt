Title: Measurement of Wi-Fi Signal Strength vs Distance for Wi-Fi Router

AIM:
To measure Wi-Fi signal strength at different distances from a Wi-Fi router and study the relationship between distance and received signal strength.

SOFTWARE:
WiFiMan application and Python (Matplotlib).

THEORY:
Wi-Fi uses radio waves to transmit data between a router and wireless devices. Signal strength is measured using RSSI (Received Signal Strength Indicator) in dBm. RSSI values are usually negative. A value closer to 0 dBm indicates a stronger signal. As distance from the router increases, the signal generally becomes weaker.

RSSI SIGNAL QUALITY:
-30 to -50 dBm : Excellent
-51 to -60 dBm : Very Good
-61 to -70 dBm : Good
-71 to -80 dBm : Fair
Below -80 dBm : Weak

PROCEDURE:
1. Switch on the Wi-Fi router.
2. Connect the laptop/mobile to the Wi-Fi network.
3. Open the WiFiMan application and identify the Wi-Fi network (SSID).
4. Observe the RSSI value near the router.
5. Move to different distances and record the RSSI value.
6. Enter the readings in the observation table.
7. Plot the graph of distance versus RSSI using Python.

PYTHON PROGRAM:
import matplotlib.pyplot as plt

distance = [0, 3.75, 7.5, 11.25, 15, 18.75, 22.5]
rssi = [-37, -51, -60, -62, -69, -74, -76]

plt.plot(distance, rssi, marker='o')
plt.xlabel("Distance from Wi-Fi Router (meters)")
plt.ylabel("RSSI (dBm)")
plt.title("Distance vs Wi-Fi Signal Strength")
plt.grid(True)
plt.show()

FACTORS AFFECTING WI-FI SIGNAL:
1. Distance: Greater distance generally gives a weaker signal.
2. Obstacles: Walls, doors and furniture reduce signal strength.
3. Interference: Other Wi-Fi networks and electronic devices can affect the signal.
4. Frequency: 2.4 GHz, 5 GHz and 6 GHz bands have different coverage and penetration.
5. Antenna: Antenna position and direction affect signal reception.

RESULT:
Wi-Fi signal strength was measured at different distances from the router and a graph of distance versus RSSI was plotted.

CONCLUSION:
As the distance from the router increases, the RSSI value generally becomes more negative, which means the Wi-Fi signal becomes weaker. Obstacles and interference can also reduce signal strength.
